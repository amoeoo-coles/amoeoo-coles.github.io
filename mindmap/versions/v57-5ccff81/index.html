🧠 MindMap

A map of the Universal Provisioning Infrastructure as Code demo. Click a coloured branch to zoom in; click a page name to open it.

Tip: drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, use Back or Esc to zoom out, and press / to search. The coloured chips under the map jump to that branch; click the active chip or Home to return to the overview. Use Zoom in, Zoom out and Fit to screen, or A− and A+ to change text size. The ↗ on a branch opens that page without zooming in. The light/dark toggle in the top-right inverts the page; the map stays readable in both themes.
