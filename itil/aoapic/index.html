1. Service overview & catalog

Service: Cisco ACI fabric for the Always-On DevNet APIC sandbox (AOAPIC). Endpoint: https://sandboxapicdc.cisco.com. Source of model: live.

What it provides: tenant networking (VRFs, bridge domains, subnets, app profiles, EPGs), fabric and access policy, and a Network-as-Code export used by nac-test and provisioning demos.
