Glossary

TermMeaning on this site
ACIApplication Centric Infrastructure — Cisco’s SDN fabric controlled by the APIC.
APICApplication Policy Infrastructure Controller — the controller cluster for an ACI fabric.
AOAPICAlways-On DevNet APIC sandbox at sandboxapicdc.cisco.com (shared demo; read-only for this bot unless Aung approves a write).
ARRReservable DevNet APIC simulator (commonly 10.10.20.14). Needs its reservation VPN; often red on Status when the VPN is down.
BDBridge Domain — L2 forwarding construct inside a VRF in ACI.
CMDBConfiguration Management Database — here, NetBox (with the ACI plugin) is the source of truth.
EPGEndpoint Group — policy group for endpoints in an ACI application profile.
ITILIT Infrastructure Library — service-management practices (incident, problem, change, etc.) used on the ITIL pages.
L3OutLayer-3 outside connection from a tenant VRF to an external network.
NaC / nac-aciNetwork as Code — YAML model of ACI policy consumed by Cisco nac-aci tooling.
nac-testTest tool that checks an APIC against an intended nac-aci model / defaults.
NetBoxDCIM/IPAM source of truth used here with netbox_aci_plugin.
VRFVirtual Routing and Forwarding instance inside an ACI tenant.
GoatCounterPrivacy-friendly hit counter feeding the Visitors pages.
RAGRed / Amber / Green status used on the Status page.

FAQ

Is anything written to the APIC from these pages?

No. Pages are generated from read-only pulls and NetBox GETs. Fabric writes need Aung’s explicit approval in chat for a specific change.

How often do pages update?

Most monitoring, NetBox, model, status, visitors, ITIL and wiki pages are regenerated by the Report 17 / drilldown.py daily build (and on demand when a change is published). Timestamps on each page are Melbourne (AEDT/AEST).

Why is Simulator APIC red?

ARR at 10.10.20.14 is unreachable until the DevNet reservation VPN is connected. The ARR model and ARR ITIL pages then fall back to the 28 Oct export and say so.

Where do I suggest a change?

Use the top-right “Have an idea? Suggest on GitHub” link on any page, or the RoadMap suggestion link.

What does the number badge on a main-menu icon mean?

It is the count of sub pages in that group (from the G array in site_inject.py).

Why do some ITIL sections say “n/a”?

The generators never invent incidents or percentages. If history.json has too few samples, availability % stays n/a with an explanation.
