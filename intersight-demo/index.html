🔭 Intersight Demo

This page group pulls out every Cisco Intersight part of the Demo AI Pod walk-through, a companion to Cisco Live LABCOM-2009 “Simplifying AI deployments with Cisco AI Pod”. In the demo, Intersight is the first stop of Task 1. You use it to look at the Cisco UCS X-Series hardware under the AI POD (power, servers, fabric interconnects, chassis and GPUs) before moving on to Red Hat OpenShift [4] [1].

Intersight is about 5 numbered steps in a 10-minute task, so the demo content is short. These pages keep the source's own steps and expected results, add the screenshots from the demo guide, and fill in context from the Cisco AI POD design guide. Nothing here is made up: topics the demo does not cover are marked Not in demo.

Summary: what Intersight does in the AI POD demo

Manages the physical layer

In the demo, Cisco Intersight manages the physical UCS infrastructure, while Red Hat OpenShift hosts the containerized applications [1].

Signed in automatically

From the demo landing page, Go to Intersight signs you in automatically [1].

Power & Energy

The first view is the Power & Energy dashboard: power consumption and overall energy use of the managed UCS servers [1].

Inventory of the AI POD

Operate > Servers, Fabric Interconnects and Chassis show UCS X210c M7 compute nodes paired with NVIDIA A100 GPUs in the X440p PCIe enclosure [1].

GPU check

On a UCSX-210C-M7 server, Inventory > GPUs confirms the 2 NVIDIA A100 GPUs installed for your session [1].

In the design guide

Intersight gives SaaS-based lifecycle management (with an on-premises appliance option), policy-driven configuration, health monitoring, firmware management and automation for all UCS compute in an AI POD [2] [3].

Demo environment (from the source)

  • Cisco AI PODs for Inferencing, a Cisco Validated Design on the Cisco UCS X-Series Modular System. Each attendee gets their own Red Hat OpenShift AI project with a dedicated NVIDIA A100 GPU [5].
  • Hardware seen in Intersight: Cisco UCS X9508 chassis, UCS X210c M7 compute nodes with NVIDIA A100 GPUs in the UCS X440p PCIe node, and Fabric Interconnects [6] [1].
  • Every Cisco AI POD includes Cisco Intersight, alongside 9108 Intelligent Fabric Modules, UCS 6536 Fabric Interconnects, an NVIDIA AI Enterprise subscription and Red Hat OpenShift licensing [7].
  • The guide's screenshots come from the RTP DC demo instance. A live session (Singapore DC) has different server and object names [5].

Reports from real Intersight data

📑 Intersight Reports turn the SNG-AI1 Intersight CSV exports (uploaded 12 Nov 2026) into report pages: server profiles, domain profiles, chassis profiles, servers, chassis, Fabric Interconnects, NetApp storage, targets, the software repository, tags, sites and certificates and licenses, with check results, a relationship diagram and a dataset inventory.

Not covered by the demo

Not in demo The demo does not create server profiles or policies, upgrade firmware, install an OS or deploy Kubernetes from Intersight. It is a read-only tour of what is already deployed. Existing server, domain and chassis profiles now have real data on the Reports pages, but creating them is still not covered. For those tasks, see the Intersight Help Center [8]. The AI POD design page lists which of these the design guide says are available or planned for each AI POD server.
