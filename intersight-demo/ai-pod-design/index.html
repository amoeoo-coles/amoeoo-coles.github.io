Intersight in the AI POD design

ObjectivePut the demo in context: what the Cisco AI POD design guide expects Intersight to do in a production AI POD [5] [4].

Role in the management framework

  • Design guide Compute infrastructure management. Intersight provides centralized, cloud-managed lifecycle management for all Cisco UCS compute, including the GPU nodes and management cluster servers. It is SaaS-based, with an on-premises appliance option [3] [4].
  • Design guide What it does. Policy-driven configuration, health monitoring, firmware management and automation [4].
  • Design guide Split of duties. Nexus Dashboard manages the network and Intersight manages compute, which together make the end-to-end management framework [3] [4].
  • Design guide Scaling. As you add Scale Units, Nexus Dashboard and Intersight keep deployment easy and operations consistent across the expanded infrastructure [5].
  • Design guide Monitoring. Intersight gives deep visibility into the health, status and alarms of UCS servers, including power, temperature and fan speed. The guide's Splunk dashboard takes AI POD infrastructure health data directly from Intersight and the Nexus switches [5].
The design guide notes that Intersight support for new hardware such as the UCS C8xx series changes over time. Always check the latest Intersight documentation for what these servers support [5] [6].

Versions and licensing

  • In the guide's validated software list, Cisco Intersight is a SaaS platform with no version (N/A). The UCS X210c M7 compute nodes are at 5.2(2.240080), and the UCS X Direct 100G (UCSX-S9108-100G) is at 4.3(5.240162) [2].
