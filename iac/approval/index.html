Universal Provisioning: Infrastructure as Code Demo › Flow step 5

Approval

No write happens until a named approver has reviewed the plan and approved it.

Explanation

The approver sees the request, the generated model, the plan (what will change) and the test results in one place. Approval is recorded against the request, with who approved it and when.

Approval is environment-specific: a change approved for Development still needs its own approval for Production. A fresh drift check runs right before the write, so an approval never applies to a fabric that has changed since it was reviewed.
