ACI Lab Guide Policy

A single, de-duplicated Network-as-Code data model for the Cisco Live LTRDCN-2143 pod1 lab. Every stanza is taken from the step pages of the ACI Lab Guide on this site, then merged under apic: — access policies, the VMware VMM domain, and the aci_p01_tenant tenant (VRF, bridge domains including mgmt and L2, DHCP relay, L3Out, filters, contracts, application profiles, EPGs, ESGs and microsegmentation). The Multi-Site orchestrator step also contributes msite_p01_tenant. Credentials are placeholders; no lab passwords are stored. This page is documentation only. No APIC writes are performed.
