ACI Policy

ACI Policy

The ACI policy model as a colour-coded tree. Every box is a subpage (APIC class, DN, GUI path, NaC and REST examples). Policy Tenant is a layered worked example of one tenant; ACI GUI Forms documents the APIC dialogs.

Pod Policy Group is an extra child of Fabric, Access, Inventory. Policy Tenant is linked from Tenants as a worked example. Alias URLs: /aci-policy/l3out/ redirects to Outside Network, /aci-policy/vmm-domain/ redirects to VM Domains.
