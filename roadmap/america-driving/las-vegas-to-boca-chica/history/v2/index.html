Las Vegas to Boca Chica

v2 · 22 Nov 2026, 4:49 PM AEDT

A sourced road-trip plan from Las Vegas, Nevada, to Boca Chica Beach and Starbase, Texas, via Kingman, Flagstaff, Phoenix, Tucson, El Paso, Van Horn, Fort Stockton, San Antonio and Brownsville. Distances and drive times are from the public OSRM driving router on OpenStreetMap, retrieved 22 Nov 2026, and are driving time only — they do not include fuel stops, meals, traffic, weather, checkpoints, park visits or overnight stops. Times and distances are measured between the Nominatim place centroids listed below, not a specific hotel or airport terminal. Travel dates are not set. CES 2027 in Las Vegas, 6–9 January 2027, is a possible start point, not a booked itinerary.

Total (OSRM, 22 Nov 2026): 1680.9 miles · 2705.2 km · about 29 hours 52 min of driving.

The routed path follows US-93 / I-11 to Kingman, I-40 to Flagstaff, I-17 to Phoenix, I-10 through Tucson, El Paso, Van Horn, Fort Stockton and San Antonio, then I-37 and US-77 / I-69E to Brownsville and TX-4 to Boca Chica Beach. That Flagstaff loop is longer than a direct Las Vegas–Phoenix–Tucson run on US-93 / I-10. Going via Flagstaff is the route this page measures, because it is the one specified for this trip.

Day plans

The whole drive is about 29 hours 52 min. A three-day crossing is possible only with long driving days — the third day is about 11 hours at the wheel. The four-day plan eases the first two days and leaves a shorter last day into Boca Chica, with a still-long third day of about 9 hours. Hotel availability is not collected.

Three-day plan (long days)

Day 1

Las Vegas to Tucson

508.6 mi · 818.5 km · 9 hours 23 min

Via Kingman, Flagstaff and Phoenix. Overnight in Tucson. A Hoover Dam stop adds only a short driving detour (see below). A Grand Canyon South Rim visit is a large add-on and would usually mean overnighting in Flagstaff instead.

Day 2

Tucson to Fort Stockton

558.9 mi · 899.5 km · 9 hours 29 min

I-10 through El Paso and Van Horn. Overnight in Fort Stockton. White Sands or Carlsbad Caverns can be added only if you accept a much longer day or a later arrival.

Day 3

Fort Stockton to Boca Chica

613.4 mi · 987.2 km · 11 hours 00 min

I-10 to San Antonio, then I-37 and US-77 / I-69E to Brownsville and TX-4 to the beach. This is a very long day. San Antonio is a pass-through on this plan, not a visit.

Four-day relaxed plan

Day 1

Las Vegas to Phoenix

395.7 mi · 636.8 km · 7 hours 21 min

Via Kingman and Flagstaff. Overnight in Phoenix. Optional Hoover Dam on the way. For the Grand Canyon South Rim, overnight in Flagstaff instead and continue to Phoenix the next morning.

Day 2

Phoenix to El Paso

431.0 mi · 693.6 km · 7 hours 33 min

I-10 through Tucson. Overnight in El Paso. White Sands is a substantial extra on this day.

Day 3

El Paso to San Antonio

552.8 mi · 889.7 km · 9 hours 02 min

I-10 via Van Horn and Fort Stockton. Still a long day. Overnight in San Antonio. Carlsbad Caverns adds more than three hours if you leave I-10 for the park and rejoin at Fort Stockton.

Day 4

San Antonio to Boca Chica

301.4 mi · 485.1 km · 5 hours 57 min

Time in the morning for the Alamo or the River Walk, then I-37 and US-77 / I-69E to Brownsville and TX-4. Check Highway 4 and beach closures before the last stretch. Starbase is a short hop from the beach when the road is open.

Road tips

Fuel gaps in West Texas

I-10 east of El Paso has long stretches between the towns used as waypoints: El Paso to Van Horn is 121.2 mi (2 hours 03 min), and Van Horn to Fort Stockton is 119.7 mi (1 hour 55 min). Tucson to El Paso is 318.1 mi (5 hours 31 min). Fuel and services at unnamed exits were not collected. Fill up in the cities in the leg table before those stretches. Smartraveller also advises taking extra water in desert areas and where petrol stations can be scarce.

Border Patrol checkpoints

This route passes official U.S. Border Patrol interior checkpoints. The Sierra Blanca immigration checkpoint is a permanent station on Interstate 10 in Hudspeth County, about 90 miles east of El Paso (CBP news release, 21 January 2026; see also the Sierra Blanca Station page). Eastbound El Paso to Van Horn uses this corridor. The Sarita checkpoint is operated by Kingsville Station on U.S. Highway 77 North, one of the two main routes out of the Rio Grande Valley (Kingsville Station). That is the US-77 / I-69E corridor used between San Antonio and Brownsville; CBP describes it for traffic leaving the Valley, so southbound timing can differ from the northbound checkpoint. The Falfurrias checkpoint is on U.S. Highway 281, 13 miles south of Falfurrias, and is manned 24 hours a day (Falfurrias Station). US-281 is a parallel Valley route, not the I-37 / US-77 routing measured here. Smartraveller says to be prepared to show documents proving you are in the United States legally. Carry your passport and any visa or ESTA papers. Exact wait times are not collected.

Time zones

Most of Arizona stays on Mountain Standard Time all year and does not use daylight saving (Arizona Revised Statutes § 1-242). The Navajo Nation is the usual exception; this route’s I-40 and I-17 legs through Kingman, Flagstaff and Phoenix do not go there. In January, daylight saving is not in effect in the states that observe it, so Nevada is on Pacific Standard Time and is one hour behind Arizona. New Mexico is also on Mountain Standard Time in January, so the Arizona–New Mexico line on I-10 does not change the clock in January. In Texas, the legal Mountain / Central boundary follows the east line of Hudspeth County (49 CFR § 71.7). On I-10 that crossing is west of Van Horn: El Paso and Hudspeth County are on Mountain Time; Culberson County (Van Horn) and the rest of this Texas route are on Central Time, one hour ahead, all year.

January weather

Flagstaff sits high on the Colorado Plateau, and January snow and ice are normal there. NOAA NCEI 1991–2020 monthly normals for Flagstaff Pulliam Airport (station USW00003103) give a January snowfall normal of 20.9 inches, about 6.2 days with at least 0.1 inch of snow, a normal daily maximum of 43.4°F and a normal daily minimum of 17.6°F. Check forecasts and Arizona 511 before using I-40 and I-17. January conditions for the other cities on this route are not collected on this page.

Highway 4 and Boca Chica Beach closures

Cameron County can temporarily close State Highway 4 (from FM 1419 / Oklahoma Avenue to the beach entrance) and Boca Chica Beach for SpaceX flight or testing, under a 15 March 2019 Commissioners’ Court order. Notices are posted on the county’s Boca Chica & Highway 4 Closures page. The City of Starbase also publishes live beach and road status at Beach and Road Access. For updates, both pages say to text BEACH to 866-513-3475. Retrieved 22 Nov 2026, the City of Starbase page reported the beach open and no road delay, with no public notice then posted; that status changes, so re-check before you drive out on TX-4. The county page can lag behind the city status page.

Rental-car one-way fees

One-way rental fees, drop charges and one-way availability from Las Vegas to Brownsville or a nearby airport are not collected. Ask the rental company. Smartraveller notes that some rental companies require an International Driving Permit and that digital licences are not yet widely accepted, so carry a physical licence.

Speed limits

Posted signs govern. TxDOT states that Texas law sets a 70 mph maximum, and that the Texas Transportation Commission may set 75 mph, or 80 or 85 mph if the highway is designed for that speed and a study finds it safe and reasonable. An 80 mph limit is also permitted on parts of Interstate 10 and Interstate 20 in 10 named counties. Texas Transportation Code § 545.353 lists those I-10 / I-20 counties, including Hudspeth, Culberson, Pecos, Kerr and Kimble, which this I-10 corridor passes. Which specific segments are actually posted 75 or 80 mph is not collected. Whether any 85 mph posting exists on this route is also not collected (the 85 mph authority is for certain designed highways; this page does not treat that as a posted limit here). Speed limits in Nevada, Arizona and New Mexico are not collected on this page; obey the posted signs.

Australian licence and International Driving Permit

Smartraveller (still current at 22 November 2026) says some US states allow driving on an Australian licence for a limited time, and others also require an International Driving Permit. Some rental companies require an IDP. Get the IDP before leaving Australia, carry a physical licence, and contact the rental company and the motor vehicle department in each state you will drive in. Driving without a required IDP can void travel and vehicle insurance. Texas DPS describes driving-privilege reciprocity for a valid, unexpired foreign licence for up to one year or until the person becomes a Texas resident, under listed international agreements, and says a valid licence or international permit must be translated into English. This page does not assert which convention list Australia sits on; confirm with DPS and with the Nevada, Arizona and New Mexico motor vehicle departments. Those three states’ exact visitor-licence rules are not collected here beyond the Smartraveller advice to check each state.
