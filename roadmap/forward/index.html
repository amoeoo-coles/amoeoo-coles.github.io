အနှစ်ချုပ် / Summary

Most of the source-of-truth, monitoring, demo-site and AI-resource work is already marked Done (23 of 36). What is still open sits in four places: Cursor_tenant5 (unknown-unicast choice, contracts and DMZ L3Out), pull requests for existing model branches, ACI Rack Rental APIC access and the Simulator APIC VPN, the separate ACI hardware site, and the later full IaC flow plus extra environments. Six of the open items are suggested as Now because they are already Waiting on Aung, or — in the case of the Cursor_tenant5 contracts — a prepare-only package is already on the Pending Provisioning page. One item is suggested as Next (publish the hardware pages once the account exists). Six Phase 6 and Phase 7 items stay Later.
