American Railways

v1 · 24 Nov 2026, 9:21 PM AEDT

A hidden study page for the six Amtrak routes shown on a western long-distance route map: the Empire Builder, Coast Starlight, California Zephyr, Southwest Chief, Heartland Flyer and Sunset Limited. Facts below are checked against official Amtrak route pages, the Amtrak timetable PDFs current on 24 Nov 2026, and the official Amtrak Mileage Record dated 17 Jun 2025. Anything not taken from a cited source is marked not collected.

Route map

The six routes are drawn from OpenStreetMap relation geometry, simplified for the web. Tiles © OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL). If a line fails to load, the note under the map will say so; the table and subpages remain the source for facts.

California Zephyr

Trains 5 / 6

Colorado Rockies, Sierra Nevada, Denver, Salt Lake City, connections to San Francisco from Emeryville

Southwest Chief

Trains 3 / 4

Chicago – Los Angeles

Raton Pass, New Mexico pueblos and high desert, Flagstaff / Grand Canyon Thruway, Kansas City

Heartland Flyer

Trains 821 / 822

Oklahoma City – Fort Worth

A daily Oklahoma–Texas link, with Texas Eagle connections at Fort Worth

Sunset Limited

Trains 1 / 2

New Orleans – Los Angeles

Bayou country, southwestern deserts, Mexican border, California mountains; Phoenix connection from Maricopa
