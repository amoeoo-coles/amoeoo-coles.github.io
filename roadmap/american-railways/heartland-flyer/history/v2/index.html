Heartland Flyer — trains 821 / 822, Oklahoma City – Fort Worth. Facts are from official Amtrak sources cited at the bottom of this page.

Overview

The Heartland Flyer is Amtrak’s daily state-supported train between Oklahoma City and Fort Worth. The official route page says the service connects Oklahoma City to Fort Worth daily and that, at Fort Worth, connections are available to Dallas, Chicago and San Antonio via the Texas Eagle. The page also states that the service is financed primarily through funds made available by the Oklahoma and Texas Departments of Transportation.

The submitted map labeled the route Oklahoma City to Fort Worth and showed train 821. Official numbers are 821 and 822. On the 24 Nov 2026 timetable, train 821 is timed from Oklahoma City (8:25a) to Fort Worth (12:27p) and train 822 from Fort Worth (5:25p) to Oklahoma City (9:27p).

  • Train numbers: 821 / 822
  • Endpoints: Oklahoma City, OK – Fort Worth, TX
  • Frequency: Daily, in both directions (official route page and the 24 Nov 2026 timetable).
  • Duration: 4 hours 14 minutes on the official route page. The 24 Nov 2026 timetable prints 4h 2m for both train 821 and train 822.
  • Distance: Official mileage is not collected: the Heartland Flyer is not listed in the 17 Jun 2025 Amtrak Mileage Record retrieved for this build. Wikipedia’s List of Amtrak routes and the OpenStreetMap Texas/Railroads wiki both publish 206 miles; treat that figure as secondary until an official Amtrak mileage line is collected.
