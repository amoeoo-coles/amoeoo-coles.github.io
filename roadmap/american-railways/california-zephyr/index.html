California Zephyr — trains 5 / 6, Chicago – Emeryville (San Francisco). Facts are from official Amtrak sources cited at the bottom of this page.

Overview

The California Zephyr is Amtrak’s daily long-distance train between Chicago and Emeryville (for San Francisco), via Omaha, Denver and Salt Lake City. The official route page describes the climb through the Rockies and the Sierra Nevada and notes connecting bus service from Emeryville into San Francisco and Oakland.

Train 5 runs west toward Emeryville; train 6 runs east toward Chicago. Both are Daily on the 24 Nov 2026 timetable. The same timetable lists the intermediate rail stations used in the table below, including Grand Junction, Glenwood Springs, Denver, Omaha and Lincoln — stations also labeled on the submitted western-route map.

  • Train numbers: 5 / 6
  • Endpoints: Chicago, IL – Omaha, NE – Denver, CO – Salt Lake City, UT – Emeryville, CA (San Francisco)
  • Frequency: Daily, in both directions (timetable dated 24 Nov 2026).
  • Duration: 51 hours 20 minutes on the official route page. The 24 Nov 2026 timetable prints 52h 14m for train 6 and 52h 57m for train 5.
  • Distance: 2,438 miles Chicago–Emeryville (trains 5/6), official Amtrak Mileage Record dated 17 Jun 2025
