ACI Multi-Pod

ACI Multi-Pod

A single Cisco APIC cluster managing multiple leaf-and-spine pods that connect through an Inter-Pod Network (IPN). This page group follows Cisco CVD-style design from Cisco Live BRKDCN-2949, with planning, migration and deployment steps from the Multi-Pod configuration white paper. Every number below is quoted from those sources.

Sources

  • BRKDCN-2949 — Cisco Live EMEA 2025 session “Cisco ACI Multi-Pod Design and Deployment” by John Weston. Extracted from the published PDF (79 content slides). Slide numbers below refer to the footer of that deck.
  • DCLessons Multi-Pod Overview — architecture overview, use cases, control-plane split and topology options.
  • Cisco Multi-Pod Configuration White Paper (c11-739714) — configuration white paper, updated 21 September 2021. This is where the NX-OS example and the Add Pod wizard steps come from.
Where the 2021 configuration paper and the 2025 deck disagree, both statements are shown with their date. The most visible examples: the 2021 paper says spine switches cannot be connected back to back and that OSPF is the only protocol between the IPN and the spines; BRKDCN-2949 (2025) documents back-to-back spines from ACI 5.2(3) and OSPF or BGP underlay peering.
