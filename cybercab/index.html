CyberCab

🚕 Tesla Cybercab

Cybercab is Tesla's purpose-built two-seat robotaxi. It has no steering wheel or pedals and drives on Tesla's camera-based Full Self-Driving software inside the Tesla Robotaxi ride-hail network. It was unveiled on 10 Oct 2024, entered production at Giga Texas in April 2026 and began carrying paying riders in Austin on 3–4 Sep 2026. This group of pages keeps confirmed facts apart from Tesla's targets, and every figure has a source and a date.

Confirmed = reported as having happened, by a filing, regulator or more than one outlet. Tesla claim = a target or statement by Tesla or Elon Musk that hasn't been independently checked. Estimate = third-party count or tracker. Not published = no public figure found.

What is still unknown

  • Not published How many Cybercabs Tesla has built in total. Tesla gives installed capacity, not a Cybercab build count [20] [22].
  • Not published A consumer price, order date or ownership terms [9] [8].
  • Not published How Tesla certified the Cybercab to FMVSS rules that assume a steering wheel, pedals and mirrors. Tesla asked NHTSA to keep its whole answer confidential [5].
  • Not published Any Australian on-road date. In Australia the Cybercab is on display only. Tesla has asked for driverless trials from 2027 [23] [24].
