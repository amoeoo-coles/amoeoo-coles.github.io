Production

Cybercab production

Cybercab is built at Gigafactory Texas near Austin, alongside Model Y and Cybertruck. The first unit came off the line in February 2026 and continuous production started in April. The bottleneck has moved from building cars to putting them into service, and Tesla's federal safety certification is under an open NHTSA audit.

The unboxed process

Tesla's "unboxed" method builds the car as separate modules (front, rear, sides, floor and centre) that are fitted out and tested in parallel, then joined on a short final line, instead of moving one painted body down a long line. Reporting from the October 2026 Munro Live line tour with Lars Moravy says final assembly has about 25 stations, which is a fifth to a tenth of a Model Y line, and that the line is about 78% automated [10] [11]. Tesla also showed gold Cybercab bodies on the line in its Q2 2026 deck [6].

These line figures come from a factory tour video and press write-ups, not a Tesla filing. Treat them as reported, not audited.
