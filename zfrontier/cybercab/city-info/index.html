🇦🇺 Australia and Melbourne

  • Display only Melbourne display, 5–18 Nov 2026. Reported schedule: Richmond 5–7 Nov, Mulgrave 8–11 Nov, Nunawading 12–14 Nov (on now) and Sunshine 15–18 Nov. Tesla's own post gave no venue or hours, so check the Tesla AU/NZ event page before you go [11] [12] [13].
  • It's a static display only, with no rides or test drives. Tesla's Sunshine event lists separate Model 3 and Model Y FSD (Supervised) test drives [11] [12].
  • Tesla claim Tesla has asked the federal government to back an autonomous-vehicle trial in 2027 and to bring forward the national Autonomous Vehicle Safety Law, which is expected around 2029 [14] [15].
  • No service No Tesla Robotaxi service, driverless trial or Cybercab sale date for Australia has been announced [14] [11].
