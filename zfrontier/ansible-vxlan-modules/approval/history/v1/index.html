About these templates

One click-to-approve provisioning email for each of the 129 items in Ansible VXLAN EVPN Catalogue. Each email follows the ACI NaC Pipeline approval layout: status banner, six-stage pipeline (Pre-test, Approval PENDING, Apply, Post-test, Drift, Post-backup), a context box, Approve and Deny buttons that open a reply to 179172@gmail.com with APPROVE or REJECT, an original fabric topology and logical view, design values and a pre-test summary.

Templates only: no email is sent and no controller is contacted from these pages. Values are the catalogue's example values; credentials are placeholders; pre-test, health and fault results are not collected (template). Each item has a web preview and a downloadable email.html.
