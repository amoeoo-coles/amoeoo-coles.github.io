New Jersey Surroundings Visit Plan

v1 · 26 Nov 2026, 6:30 PM AEDT

New Jersey is easy to explore from New York by NJ Transit or car. History is everywhere: Washington’s winter camps at Morristown, Thomas Edison’s labs in West Orange, and the Great Falls that powered industrial Paterson. In summer, the Jersey Shore runs 130 miles down to Victorian Cape May. For the Hudson waterfront, see NYC to New Jersey.

Day-by-day itinerary (1, 2 or 3 days)

Pick the column that matches your time. Times are rough estimates for planning, not timetables; opening hours, closures and timed-entry rules change, so check each official site before you go.

1 day — history near NYC

  1. Morning: Thomas Edison National Historical Park (lab and Glenmont estate — check tour times).
  2. Afternoon: Paterson Great Falls National Historical Park.
  3. Or swap in Morristown NHP (Ford Mansion, Jockey Hollow).

2 days — add Princeton

  1. Day 1 as above.
  2. Day 2: NJ Transit to Princeton Junction (Dinky shuttle to campus) — Princeton University campus and art museum, Nassau Street.
  3. Afternoon: Grounds For Sculpture in Hamilton.

3 days — add the Shore

  1. Days 1–2 as above.
  2. Day 3 (summer): Asbury Park boardwalk, or a longer drive to Cape May’s Victorian streets and lighthouse.
  3. Autumn alternative: Delaware Water Gap hikes and foliage.

Getting around

  • NJ Transit trains and buses — njtransit.com (Northeast Corridor to Princeton; North Jersey Coast Line to Asbury Park).
  • Car for national parks and the Water Gap; the Garden State Parkway and NJ Turnpike are tolled.

Seasonal notes

The Shore season runs roughly Memorial Day to Labor Day; beaches may need badges in some towns (check official site). Autumn foliage peaks in October in the north. Winters are cold; some shore businesses close off-season.
