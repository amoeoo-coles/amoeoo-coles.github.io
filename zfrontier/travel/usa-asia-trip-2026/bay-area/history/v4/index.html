Day-by-day plan with Option A and Option B

Sun 13 Dec · Arrival

Fiji Airways FJXXX (Economy) leaves Melbourne at 1:55 PM on Sunday 13 December and arrives Nadi at 7:45 PM (4 h 50 m). After a 2 h 15 m transit, FJXXX (Economy) leaves Nadi at 10:00 PM and, crossing the International Date Line, lands at SFO at 12:25 PM the same calendar day (ticketed, confirmed). Keep the first afternoon easy.

  • SFO to Union Square is about 14 mi (23 km), 25 min by car (OSRM free-flow); BART also serves the airport — use the BART trip planner.
  • Option A (easy walk): the Ferry Building Marketplace and the Embarcadero waterfront. Option B: rest and an early night to beat jet lag before a driving day.
  • Book Alcatraz (16 Dec) and, if you drive to Muir Woods, the parking reservation now — see the day cards below.

Mon 14 Dec · Day trip 1

Two very different ways to use a Monday: a drive north into the redwoods, or public transport across the Bay.

Option A

Muir Woods + Sausalito

Drive: 17 mi (27 km), about 35 min each way (OSRM/OpenStreetMap free-flow estimate: no traffic, no snow delays) · Sausalito is 9 mi (15 km), 20 min from the city.

  • Muir Woods National Monument: old-growth coast redwoods with flat boardwalk loops.
  • Every vehicle needs a parking reservation in advance and reservations are not sold on site; there is no cell service at the park, so download the booking first.
  • Then Sausalito for lunch and bay views (the Golden Gate Ferry also links Sausalito with the city).

Winter / date note: The Muir Woods shuttle is not running this winter and resumes in spring 2027 (per the official reservation site), so the only way in by vehicle is a parking reservation. Reservations can be booked up to 90 days ahead, with a smaller batch released 3 days out.

Muir Woods reservations (official) · Golden Gate Ferry · OSM · Google · Google Maps directions

Option B

Berkeley + Oakland by BART

Transit: BART from downtown San Francisco (use the official planner for times). Driving reference: Berkeley 14 mi (22 km), 30 min; Oakland 11 mi (17 km), 20 min.

  • UC Berkeley campus and the Campanile (Sather Tower) — see visit.berkeley.edu.
  • Then Oakland: Jack London Square, the USS Hornet museum ship and Lake Merritt; details on our Oakland visit plan.
  • No parking reservation and no car needed — the lower-effort choice after a long flight.

Winter / date note: Rain is the main risk in December (11.6 wet days a month), but both stops have indoor options. Museum opening days differ — check each site for Monday hours.

BART · USS Hornet · Oakland visit plan (zFrontier) · OSM · Google · Google Maps directions

Tue 15 Dec · Day trip 2

A wildlife day on the Pacific coast, or a long run south to Monterey Bay.

Option A

Año Nuevo State Park elephant seals (+ Half Moon Bay)

Drive: 57 mi (91 km), about 1 h 30 min each way (OSRM/OpenStreetMap free-flow estimate: no traffic, no snow delays) · Half Moon Bay is on the way: 30 mi (49 km), 45 min.

  • Northern elephant seals haul out here to breed in winter; the park runs guided walks to the seals.
  • Guided walks start on 15 Dec — the first day of the 2026–27 season — and run daily to 31 Mar, except 25 Dec, according to the state parks listing. Reserve in advance through ReserveCalifornia.
  • Break the drive at Half Moon Bay for the coast and lunch.

Winter / date note: Winter weather on this coast can be wet and windy: bring waterproofs and sturdy shoes. Walk times and site rules are set by park staff — check the page before you book.

Año Nuevo State Park · OSM · Google · Google Maps directions

Option B

Monterey + Carmel-by-the-Sea (long day)

Drive: 114 mi (184 km), about 2 h 25 min each way (OSRM/OpenStreetMap free-flow estimate: no traffic, no snow delays) · Carmel is 117 mi (188 km), 2 h 25 min. That is about five hours of driving in total.

  • Monterey Bay Aquarium on Cannery Row, then Carmel-by-the-Sea.
  • Realistic only as a very early start; consider an overnight if you want a relaxed day.

Winter / date note: Short winter daylight (about 9.6 hours) makes the return drive after dark likely.

Monterey Bay Aquarium · OSM · Google · Google Maps directions

Wed 16 Dec · City day — last full day

Both options stay in San Francisco; pick by weather.

Option A

Alcatraz + waterfront

Alcatraz Landing at Pier 33 is 2 mi (3 km) from Union Square.

  • Alcatraz Island (NPS) — tickets are sold by the official concessioner Alcatraz City Cruises; book ahead.
  • Then Pier 39, Fisherman's Wharf and the Ferry Building.

Winter / date note: Ferries can be cancelled for weather; keep the afternoon flexible.

Alcatraz (NPS) · OSM · Google · Google Maps directions

Option B

Golden Gate Park + Presidio

The de Young Museum is 6 mi (10 km) from Union Square.

  • de Young Museum, the California Academy of Sciences and the Japanese Tea Garden in Golden Gate Park.
  • Then the Golden Gate Bridge and the Presidio — see the San Francisco visit plan.

Winter / date note: Mostly indoors in the park, so a good rain-day pick.

Golden Gate Park · OSM · Google · Google Maps directions

Thu 17 Dec · Depart

SFO to Boise at 6:50 AM (ticketed, confirmed; arrives Boise 1:20 PM Mountain Time). Arrange early transport the evening before; SFO is about 25 min from Union Square by car (free-flow).

  • Pack the winter kit where you can reach it: Boise is the longest stay and the coldest so far.
