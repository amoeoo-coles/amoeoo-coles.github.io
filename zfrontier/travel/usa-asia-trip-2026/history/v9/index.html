Safety notice for the Yangon stay and the Mandalay side trip

Australia's Smartraveller advice for Myanmar is “Do not travel” (level 4), still listed as current on 28 Nov 2026 (updated 7 April 2026), and the UK FCDO advises against all or all but essential travel to many regions — including against all but essential travel to Mandalay Region, and against all travel to the north of Mandalay Region and to Sagaing Region. Read Smartraveller and GOV.UK before committing. The Yangon and Mandalay pages list options only; they do not recommend travelling.

  • Dates and times are local. Ticketed (confirmed) legs are the 13 December Melbourne to San Francisco itinerary (Fiji Airways FJ934 and FJ870, via Nadi), the four US flights (17 Dec, 4 Jan, 9 Jan and 31 Jan, including Korean Air KE18/KE653 Los Angeles to Bangkok via Seoul Incheon), the four Amtrak trains (14, 19, 25 and 26 Jan), Thai Airways TG 301 and TG 302 (3 and 16 Feb), and Jetstar JQ30 Bangkok to Melbourne on 18 Feb. Legs 11 and 12 (Yangon ⇄ Mandalay, Myanma Railways train) are still to be confirmed on the ticket. Booking references, ticket numbers, seat numbers and fares are kept off this public page.
  • Mandalay side trip: 5–9 February 2027, inside the Yangon stay (Yangon remains the 3–16 February base). You will travel by Myanma Railways train between Yangon Central and Mandalay Central. The time is still to be confirmed on the ticket — no train number or departure has been added.
  • Date line: Melbourne to San Francisco lands the same calendar day, FJ870 lands in San Francisco at 12:25 PM on Sunday 13 December (the same calendar day), and Korean Air KE653 lands in Bangkok at 11:05 PM on Monday 1 February.
  • Overnight legs: the 9 January flight from Las Vegas lands in Tampa at 6:05 AM on Sun 10 January; the 14 January Floridian is an overnight train, departing Tampa Union Station at 4:49 PM Thursday and arriving Washington Union Station at 3:12 PM Friday (about 22 hours); and Jetstar JQ30 leaves Bangkok at 9:25 PM on Thursday 18 February and arrives Melbourne at 10:25 AM on Friday 19 February.
  • Amtrak tickets: changes are not allowed; cancellation carries a 30% fee.
  • Prices are left out on purpose. Drive distances and times are OSRM/OpenStreetMap free-flow estimates; weather is climate normals, not a forecast.
