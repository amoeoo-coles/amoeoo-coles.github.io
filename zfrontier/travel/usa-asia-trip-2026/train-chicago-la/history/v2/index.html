Chicago is the coldest stop of the whole trip, with highs around freezing, lows around −7°C and often wind. Wear the full winter kit for the Chicago layover, then expect trains to be heated and the Los Angeles arrival on 28 Jan to be mild (Los Angeles January normal 20.0°C / 9.6°C). Heavy snow can delay both trains, so see the plan-B notes below.

Day-by-day plan with Option A and Option B

Mon 25 Jan · Board the Lake Shore Limited (New York → Chicago)

Amtrak's Lake Shore Limited (train 49) runs overnight from New York to Chicago. The booked departure is 3:40 PM from Moynihan Train Hall at Penn Station (2 coach seats, same reservation as the Southwest Chief).

  • Typical route cities include Albany-Rensselaer, Syracuse, Rochester, Buffalo-Depew, Cleveland and Toledo; check Amtrak for the stops and times of your train.
  • Late-January afternoons leave little daylight for the Hudson River views at the start; the scenery is mostly dark overnight.
  • Pack an overnight kit in a small bag you can reach: layers, charger or power bank, eye mask, refillable bottle and snacks.
  • See the SWChief train page for the typical train layout (the Southwest Chief part).

Check the Amtrak app on the day for the status of train 49 — a late arrival in Chicago shortens or ends the layover.

Tue 26 Jan · Chicago layover — then the Southwest Chief

The Lake Shore Limited reaches Chicago in the morning. The Southwest Chief (train 3) leaves Chicago Union Station at 1:30 PM according to the Amtrak timetable used on our sightseeing page, so the layover is only about three hours. Stay close, wear the full winter kit and be back at the station well before departure.

Option A

Stay close: Union Station, Chicago Riverwalk, Willis Tower

Walking from Union Station: Willis Tower is 0.4 km straight line, the Riverwalk is about 1.4 km straight line.

  • Chicago Union Station and its Great Hall (the famous staircase).
  • A short Chicago Riverwalk stretch and a look at Willis Tower (Skydeck opens later — check hours).

Winter / date note: Highs around freezing, lows near −7°C, and wind: keep walks short and indoors-friendly.

Chicago visit plan (zFrontier) · OSM · Google · Walking route (Google)

Option B

Art Institute of Chicago — a highlights visit

The Art Institute is about 1.4 km straight line from Union Station — allow time to walk or use a short ride, and be back well before 1:30 PM.

  • Art Institute of Chicago: pick two or three galleries rather than the whole museum.
  • Check the museum's opening time for Tuesday 26 Jan before relying on this option.

Winter / date note: A cold walk between the station and the museum; a ride may be easier with bags.

Art Institute of Chicago · OSM · Google

Plan B: if train 49 is badly late, tell the conductor you connect to train 3 and watch the Amtrak app. Amtrak's service-disruption and missed-connection policies are on amtrak.com.

Wed 27 Jan · On the Southwest Chief — the scenic day

The official westbound timetable on our Southwest Chief sightseeing page lists day-2 stops from Newton, Kansas through to Flagstaff. Two plans for the same day.

Option A

Daylight scenery plan

From the sightseeing page's timetable (local times).

  • Morning: La Junta (6:50 AM MT) and Trinidad (8:10 AM MT), then the Raton Pass climb to Raton (9:12 AM MT).
  • Late morning to afternoon: Las Vegas, New Mexico (10:57 AM), Lamy (12:53 PM) and Albuquerque (3:14 PM).
  • Evening: Gallup, Winslow and Flagstaff (8:55 PM Arizona time); the Colorado River crossing is at night.

Winter / date note: This is a time-zone-heavy day (Central, Mountain and Arizona time); use the sightseeing page for which side to sit on.

Southwest Chief sightseeing (zFrontier)

Option B

Station-stop plan

Use longer stops to stretch your legs; dwell times come from the arrival and departure times on the sightseeing page.

  • Albuquerque has the longest daylight stop: arrive 2:24 PM, depart 3:14 PM (about 50 minutes).
  • La Junta (6:35 / 6:50 AM MT) and Lamy (12:49 / 12:53 PM MT) are shorter.
  • Always listen for the conductor's announcements; stops can change.

Winter / date note: Winter platforms can be icy and cold, so wear shoes with grip.

Amtrak Southwest Chief

For Santa Fe or Grand Canyon side trips from the train, see the sightseeing page — they are not part of this plan.

Thu 28 Jan · Arrive in Los Angeles

The Southwest Chief arrives at Los Angeles Union Station at 7:27 AM on the official timetable (Pacific Time).

  • Early-morning stops include San Bernardino (5:21 AM) and Fullerton (6:39 AM).
  • Continue with the Los Angeles day-trip plan.
