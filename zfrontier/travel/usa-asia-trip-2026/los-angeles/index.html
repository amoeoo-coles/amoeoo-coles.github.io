Day-by-day plan with Option A and Option B

Thu 28 Jan · Arrival by train

The Southwest Chief arrives at Los Angeles Union Station at 7:27 AM (ticketed, confirmed). After two nights on the train, keep the morning gentle.

Option A

Union Station + downtown

Metro from Union Station; on foot.

  • Los Angeles Union Station (1939 Mission Revival), then a downtown walk; see the Los Angeles visit plan.

Winter / date note: Check-in may not be possible this early — ask the hotel about luggage storage.

Union Station · OSM · Google

Option B

Griffith Observatory + Space Shuttle Endeavour

Griffith Observatory is 8 mi (13 km) from Union Station, 20 min by car.

  • Griffith Observatory (free; check opening days).
  • California Science Center — Space Shuttle Endeavour in the Samuel Oschin Air and Space Center, which the Science Center says opened on 13 Nov 2026.

Winter / date note: Check timed-entry rules at both sites.

California Science Center · OSM · Google · Google Maps directions

Fri 29 Jan · Day trip 1

The Pacific Coast Highway, or a long day north to Santa Barbara.

Option A

Malibu + Santa Monica

Drive: 28 mi (45 km), about 45 min each way (OSRM/OpenStreetMap free-flow estimate: no traffic, no snow delays) · Santa Monica Pier is 17 mi (27 km), 25 min.

  • Malibu and the Pacific Coast Highway; Santa Monica Pier and Pacific Park.
  • Option to add the Getty Center (18 mi (29 km) from Union Station).

Winter / date note: Cool and breezy on the coast; sunset is early.

Santa Monica Pier · OSM · Google · Google Maps directions

Option B

Santa Barbara

Drive: 95 mi (153 km), about 2 h each way (OSRM/OpenStreetMap free-flow estimate: no traffic, no snow delays) · about four hours of driving in total.

  • Santa Barbara: Stearns Wharf, the old mission and the beachfront.

Winter / date note: A long day that may return after dark; allow for traffic on the way back.

Visit Santa Barbara · OSM · Google · Google Maps directions

Sat 30 Jan · Day trip 2

A theme-park day, or an island day by ferry.

Option A

Disneyland Resort (Anaheim)

Drive: 27 mi (43 km), about 40 min each way (OSRM/OpenStreetMap free-flow estimate: no traffic, no snow delays)

  • Disneyland Resort: Disneyland Park and Disney California Adventure. Check dates, hours and ticket rules on the official site.

Winter / date note: Weekends are usually busier than weekdays; check the park calendar for crowd and hours.

Disneyland Resort · OSM · Google · Google Maps directions

Option B

Catalina Island (Avalon)

Drive to the San Pedro terminal: about 27 mi (43 km), 40 min (approximate terminal location). Ferry: about 1 h 15 min from San Pedro, about 1 h from Long Beach.

  • Catalina Express winter schedule (20 Oct 2026 – 31 Mar 2027): from Long Beach 6:00 AM, 9:50 AM, 2:00 PM and 5:50 PM, with returns from Avalon at 7:50 AM, 11:50 AM, 3:55 PM and 7:45 PM; a 9:50 AM departure and a 3:55 PM return would give about four hours on the island.
  • Walk Avalon and the harbour; the island's interior tours are separate.

Winter / date note: Crossings can be rough in winter and are weather-dependent; check the Catalina Express schedule page for the date and book ahead.

Catalina Express schedule · OSM · Google · Google Maps directions

Sun 31 Jan · Fly Los Angeles → Bangkok

Korean Air KEXXX leaves Los Angeles at 11:00 AM on Sunday 31 January and arrives Seoul Incheon at 5:35 PM on Monday 1 February (13 h 35 m). After a 1 h 30 m transit, KEXXX leaves Incheon at 7:05 PM and lands at Bangkok Suvarnabhumi at 11:05 PM on 1 February (ticketed, confirmed). The itinerary crosses the International Date Line.

  • LAX is 21 mi (34 km) from Union Station, 30 min by car (free-flow). An 11:00 AM international departure is later than the previous 8:00 AM listing, but it still needs an early start; staying near the airport on the 30th keeps the morning simpler. Check the airline for check-in closing time.
  • Entry notes for Thailand are on the Bangkok Suvarnabhumi airport transit page.
