Tampa Union Station (Amtrak) is at 601 North Nebraska Avenue, Tampa, FL 33602, matching the Amtrak station page and OpenStreetMap. Amtrak’s station page currently notes that operations are temporarily in a trailer next door (checked 29 Nov 2026). The 14 January Floridian (train 40) departs from this station at 4:49 PM.

The nearest mapped TECO Line Streetcar stop to the station is Port Tampa Bay Station (#5), about a 9-minute walk. Water Street Station (#8) is listed on the official TECO stop list at Beneficial Drive & Channelside Drive (the June 2025 TECO map labels stop #8 Amalie Arena), but a named tram stop was not found in OpenStreetMap, so it is not plotted and no walking distance is given.

Winter weather, 9–14 January

These are climate normals for the same calendar days, not a forecast; see the full US trip weather page for the method.

SnowNot expected (official January normal 0.0 cm)

The warmest US stop: light clothes, a layer for nights around 11°C and a compact rain jacket. In the 1991–2020 sample one in ten nights was at or below 5.5°C (42°F), and the coldest night was −1.6°C (29°F). Daylight averages about 10.5 hours. Gulf water is cool in January, so the beaches are for walking rather than swimming.

Day-by-day plan with Option A and Option B

Sat 9 Jan · Overnight flight

Las Vegas to Tampa departs at 10:55 PM Pacific on 9 Jan and lands at 6:05 AM Eastern on Sun 10 Jan (ticketed, confirmed; overnight red-eye). Ask the hotel about early check-in or bag storage.

  • Keep Sunday's plan flexible after the overnight flight.

Sun 10 Jan · Arrival — keep it light

The ticketed overnight flight from Las Vegas lands at 6:05 AM. Keep this first Tampa day light after the red-eye. The options below are only if you feel up to a longer outing; otherwise rest.

Option A

St. Petersburg + Clearwater Beach

Drive: 24 mi (39 km), about 30 min each way (OSRM/OpenStreetMap free-flow estimate: no traffic, no snow delays) · Clearwater Beach is 25 mi (40 km), 40 min from downtown Tampa.

  • The Dalí Museum in St. Petersburg and the St. Pete Pier and murals.
  • Then Clearwater Beach: white sand and the Pier 60 sunset — see Visit St. Pete-Clearwater.

Winter / date note: Mild and mostly dry in January; the Gulf is too cool for most swimming.

Tampa visit plan (zFrontier) · OSM · Google · Google Maps directions

Option B

Weeki Wachee Springs + Crystal River manatees

Drive: 56 mi (91 km), about 1 h 10 min each way (OSRM/OpenStreetMap free-flow estimate: no traffic, no snow delays) · Crystal River (Three Sisters Springs) is 80 mi (129 km), 1 h 40 min from Tampa.

  • Weeki Wachee Springs State Park: the spring-fed river and the mermaid shows.
  • Then Crystal River for manatee season (15 Nov–31 Mar): the Three Sisters Springs boardwalk and land area stay open to visitors, with paid admission.

Winter / date note: During manatee season the Kings Bay manatee sanctuaries are closed to all waterborne activity, and Three Sisters Springs is generally closed to paddling, swimming, snorkeling and diving. Temporary closures can also occur when manatees are present or in cold events.

Crystal River NWR (USFWS) · Three Sisters Springs (City of Crystal River) · OSM · Google · Google Maps directions

Mon 11 Jan · Tampa day

Both options are in the Tampa area.

Option A

Busch Gardens Tampa Bay

Busch Gardens is 9 mi (14 km), 15 min from the Riverwalk.

  • Busch Gardens Tampa Bay — rides and animals; a full day. Check operating days and hours for 11 Jan (some winter days are short).

Winter / date note: Cool mornings; bring a light layer.

Busch Gardens · OSM · Google · Google Maps directions

Option B

Riverwalk, Florida Aquarium and Ybor City

Downtown, linked by the TECO Line Streetcar.

  • Tampa Riverwalk, The Florida Aquarium and the historic cigar district of Ybor City.
  • Streetcar details: TECO Line Streetcar.

Winter / date note: Easy on a cooler day; good for the evening.

Tue 12 Jan · Day trip 2

The space coast, or an arts-and-beach day to the south.

Option A

Kennedy Space Center

Drive: 129 mi (207 km), about 2 h 35 min each way (OSRM/OpenStreetMap free-flow estimate: no traffic, no snow delays) · about 5 hours of driving.

  • Kennedy Space Center Visitor Complex: listed as open daily 9 AM–5 PM from 4 Jan 2027, with last entry at 4 PM.
  • Our dedicated page: Tampa to Kennedy Space Center (Space Shuttle Atlantis, the bus tour, launch viewing).
  • Launches planned for January 2027 may change; check the official launch schedule if a launch matters to you.

Winter / date note: Launch days bring heavy traffic and can change visitor-complex hours.

KSC launch schedule · OSM · Google · Google Maps directions

Option B

Sarasota: Ringling + Siesta Key

Drive: 59 mi (94 km), about 1 h 10 min each way (OSRM/OpenStreetMap free-flow estimate: no traffic, no snow delays)

  • The Ringling museum and grounds, then the beach at Siesta Key.

Winter / date note: A relaxed, low-risk day in January.

The Ringling · OSM · Google · Google Maps directions

Wed 13 Jan · Day trip 3 — or the Keys drive

You can keep to one-day trips, or give the middle of the stay to the long drive on the existing live page.

Option A

Fort De Soto Park + Gulf beaches

Drive: 38 mi (61 km), about 55 min each way (OSRM/OpenStreetMap free-flow estimate: no traffic, no snow delays)

  • Fort De Soto Park: beaches, a historic fort and kayaking.

Winter / date note: A cool-weather day: walk rather than swim.

Fort De Soto Park · OSM · Google · Google Maps directions

Option B

Tampa – Miami – Key West drive (replaces 11–13 Jan)

A 2–3 day drive, so it replaces several day-trip days; it does not fit in a single day.

  • Live page: Tampa – Miami – Key West drive — official mileages, Alligator Alley or the Tamiami Trail, the Overseas Highway, tolls and parking.
  • Check how you would return to Tampa Union Station for the 14 Jan 4:49 PM Floridian train before you commit.

Winter / date note: Plan this early: it uses most of the window.

Thu 14 Jan · Depart

Tampa to Washington on the Amtrak Floridian (train 40, coach), departing Tampa Union Station at 4:49 PM (ticketed, confirmed). It is an overnight train, about 22 hours, arriving Washington Union Station at 3:12 PM on Fri 15 Jan. Amtrak tickets cannot be changed; cancellation carries a 30% fee.

  • Option A: an easy morning on the Riverwalk. Option B: a last stop in Ybor City, then Tampa Union Station.
