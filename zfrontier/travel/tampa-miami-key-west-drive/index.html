Tampa – Miami – Key West drive

v1 · 28 Nov 2026, 11:43 AM AEDT

A two-leg drive from Tampa to Key West, going first to Miami and then down the Overseas Highway. The usual fast road is I-75 Alligator Alley; the slower scenic alternative is the Tamiami Trail (US-41) through the Everglades. From Miami, US-1 is the only highway to Key West, island-hopping across 42 bridges including the Seven Mile Bridge. Official FDOT highway mileage is 250 miles Tampa–Miami and 154 miles Miami–Key West (273 miles if you count the official Tampa–Naples and Naples–Miami legs of the Alligator Alley route). Plan on about 4 to 4.5 hours and 3.5 to 4 hours for those two legs without long stops (rough estimate). This page is a driving plan; the older Tampa to Key West — Florida Keys Drive page remains the broader Keys visit guide (sights, Dry Tortugas, seasonal notes).

How this fits the US trip

Tampa is already on the December 2026–January 2027 US trip. Headline figures on the weather page for the 9–14 Jan stay are the NOAA 1991–2020 daily-normal average at Tampa International Airport: 21.7°C (71°F) / 11.4°C (53°F), with snow not expected and about 7.1 January days with at least 0.25 mm of rain. A rare cold front can still near freezing (the 1991–2020 sample for that stay window had a night as low as −1.6°C).

Miami and Key West are not separate stays on that itinerary, but if you add this drive in January they are a little warmer, especially at night. Official January monthly normals (same 1991–2020 period, fetched 28 Nov 2026):

  • Tampa: 21.8°C (71°F) / 11.6°C (53°F), 67.3 mm over 7.1 days. December normals, if you ever shifted the drive earlier, are 23.3°C (74°F) / 13.3°C (56°F), 65.0 mm over 6.0 days.
  • Miami: January 24.6°C (76°F) / 16.1°C (61°F), 46.5 mm over 7.7 days. December 25.7°C (78°F) / 17.9°C (64°F), 62.0 mm over 8.0 days.
  • Key West: January 24.3°C (76°F) / 18.6°C (65°F), 46.5 mm over 6.9 days. December 25.4°C (78°F) / 20.2°C (68°F), 54.9 mm over 6.4 days. Key West nights stay clearly milder than Tampa.

January is dry season and peak visitor season in South Florida and the Keys: good driving weather, busier roads and fuller hotels. Official hurricane season is June–November, so it is not a factor for a January drive. Pack a light layer for a Tampa or Miami evening and a rain shell; you will not need the winter kit from the Boise or New York legs. Full packing notes stay on the US trip weather, December to January page.

The two legs

Tampa

Tampa is the 9–14 Jan 2027 stop on the US trip (US trip weather, December to January, Tampa visit plan). A Key West drive is an optional side trip from that window, not a separate stay already on the itinerary. Five days in Tampa also have to cover the city itself and, if you want it, the Tampa to Kennedy Space Center day trip, so a 2–3 day Keys run would use most of the window. Decide before you book Miami or Key West rooms.

Tampa to Miami — I-75 Alligator Alley

This is the usual fast route. Official FDOT intercity highway mileage (spreadsheet updated 14 Sep 2026) is Tampa–Miami 250 miles, or Tampa–Naples 164 miles plus Naples–Miami 109 miles (273 miles) if you follow I-75 south through Naples and then Alligator Alley. The 250-mile city-to-city figure is the official matrix value; the 273-mile sum is the Alligator Alley driving combination most people mean. Door to door, plan on about 4 to 4.5 hours without long stops (rough estimate). Consumer guides often round this to “about 280 miles”; the official numbers above are the ones used on this page.

Fill up and buy food in Fort Myers or Naples before you enter the Alley. Alligator Alley is the 78-mile limited-access stretch of I-75 across the Everglades, between Collier County (near Naples) and Broward County (toward Fort Lauderdale and Miami). Services are sparse on the Alley itself. After the east plaza, I-595 or I-95 takes you into Miami without having to use Florida’s Turnpike; the Turnpike is optional and adds more tolls.

The Tamiami Trail alternative (US-41)

From the Naples area, US-41 (the Tamiami Trail) runs east through Big Cypress and along the northern edge of Everglades National Park to Miami. It is slower, with two-lane stretches, lower speed limits and park traffic, but it is the road for a Shark Valley or Big Cypress stop. Allow about 5 to 6 hours Tampa to Miami with a park visit (rough estimate). You can also drive Alligator Alley and detour south to Shark Valley, or do the Trail one way and the Alley the other.

Shark Valley (Everglades National Park) has a 15-mile paved loop and an observation tower, by bike or on the park tram. Check hours, tram tickets and entrance fees on the National Park Service Shark Valley page. Big Cypress National Preserve is just west of there (nps.gov/bicy).

Miami to Key West — US-1 Overseas Highway

Official FDOT mileage is Miami–Key West 154 miles (155 miles the other way) and 161 miles from Miami Beach. Plan on 3.5 to 4 hours without stops, and closer to 5–6 hours with Key Largo, Islamorada, the Seven Mile Bridge and Bahia Honda (rough estimate). Weekend afternoons, holidays and peak dry-season traffic often add more. There is no alternative highway — US-1 is the road, with mile markers counting down to MM 0 in Key West.

The Overseas Highway crosses 42 bridges. The Seven Mile Bridge between Marathon and Little Duck Key is the famous over-water span (about 6.8 miles). The historic Old Seven Mile Bridge, part of Henry Flagler’s Overseas Railroad, runs beside it; access to walkways and Pigeon Key changes, so check official site before you plan a walk on the old span.

Suggested 2–3 day itinerary

Day 1 — Tampa to Miami

  1. Leave Tampa early (aim for a 7:00 AM start) to clear weekday Tampa traffic and reach Miami before the afternoon rush.
  2. Take I-75 south through Sarasota, Fort Myers and Naples. Fill the tank in Naples or Fort Myers before Alligator Alley.
  3. Cross the Everglades on Alligator Alley (I-75). There is one toll, collected once, and no cash booths — see Tolls and SunPass below.
  4. Alternative: from Naples, take the Tamiami Trail (US-41) and stop at Shark Valley or the Big Cypress visitor centre. This is slower (plan 5–6 hours Tampa–Miami with a park stop, rough estimate) but is the wildlife and boardwalk option.
  5. Arrive in Miami in the afternoon. If you want the cruise-port view from Aung’s in-car screen, drive the MacArthur Causeway over to Miami Beach and back.
  6. Overnight in Miami or Miami Beach. Do not try to add the Overseas Highway on the same day unless you left very early and will stop in the Upper Keys rather than Key West.

Day 2 — Miami to Key West on the Overseas Highway

  1. Leave Miami in the morning and join US-1 south through Florida City and Homestead. Official FDOT mileage Miami–Homestead is 29 miles, Homestead–Key West 126 miles.
  2. Stop at Key Largo (John Pennekamp Coral Reef State Park if you want time on the water) and Islamorada for lunch.
  3. Continue to Marathon, then drive the Seven Mile Bridge. There are pull-outs at each end; the historic Old Seven Mile Bridge runs alongside.
  4. If daylight remains, pull into Bahia Honda State Park for the beach and the old Bahia Honda Bridge view.
  5. Arrive in Key West in the afternoon. Park once at the city’s Old Town Parking Garage (see the parking tip below) and walk or ride the city bus. Catch the sunset gathering at Mallory Square if you like a crowd.
  6. Overnight in Key West.

Day 3 — Key West, then start the return

  1. Morning in Old Town: Southernmost Point buoy, Duval Street, the Ernest Hemingway Home & Museum, or Fort Zachary Taylor Historic State Park for a swim. Book ticketed houses on their official sites.
  2. A Dry Tortugas day trip (ferry or seaplane) is a full extra day and usually has to be booked well ahead — see the older Florida Keys drive page. It does not fit a tight 3-day Tampa turnaround.
  3. Start the drive north after lunch if you need to be back in Tampa the same night (a long day: Key West–Miami plus Miami–Tampa). Otherwise overnight again in the Middle Keys or Miami and finish the return on the morning of day 4.

A tighter 2-day option

  1. Day 1: Tampa to the Upper or Middle Keys (Key Largo, Islamorada or Marathon), taking Alligator Alley and skipping a Miami overnight, or taking Tamiami Trail with a short Everglades stop. This is a 7–8 hour day with stops (rough estimate).
  2. Day 2: Seven Mile Bridge, Bahia Honda and Key West in the morning, then start back. You will not have a full Key West day.
  3. This squeeze uses two of the five nights in the 9–14 Jan Tampa window and is tiring. The 3-day plan is the better default.

Tolls, fuel, timing and Key West parking

Tolls and SunPass

Alligator Alley is a Florida’s Turnpike Enterprise / FDOT toll road. Cash is not accepted. A two-axle car pays $3.18 with SunPass and $3.51 Toll-By-Plate, at the west plaza (eastbound) or the east plaza (westbound) — one charge, not both. Those rates are from the official South Florida toll-rate sheet effective 3 Aug 2026 (South Florida toll rates PDF). Rates are adjusted over time, so confirm before you drive.

If you stay on I-75 / I-595 / I-95 into Miami you do not have to use Florida’s Turnpike. If you do use the Turnpike or the Sawgrass Expressway, more tolls apply — use the official SunPass toll calculator.

SunPass, E-PASS, E-ZPass and several other transponders are accepted on Florida toll roads. Without a transponder, cameras bill the registered owner as Toll-By-Plate (a higher toll, plus an administrative charge on a mailed invoice). Visitors can set up a pass at sunpass.com. Rental cars often have a transponder programme — check the rental agreement so you are not billed twice.

The Overseas Highway (US-1 through the Keys) is not a toll road.

Fuel

Fill up in Fort Myers or Naples before Alligator Alley, and again in Florida City, Homestead or Key Largo before the long Keys bridges. Services on the Alley are limited, and fuel in the Keys is usually dearer than on the mainland. Keep a comfortable reserve — do not plan to arrive in Key West on fumes at night.

Best times

Leave Tampa early. Alligator Alley itself is usually free-flowing; the delays are Tampa’s morning rush and Miami’s afternoon one. On US-1, morning is calmer than weekend afternoons and holidays. Watch for lower speed limits, sudden queues at bridges, and drivers stopping for photographs.

January is the comfortable dry-season window, and also the busy one. Book Key West lodging and the Old Town garage mindset (park once) before you go. After dark the Overseas Highway is poorly lit and the views are gone; try not to be on the Seven Mile Bridge for the first time at night.

Key West parking tip

Old Town streets are narrow and parking is tight. The city’s own tip is to use the Old Town Parking Garage (Park N’ Ride) at 301 Grinnell Street, corner of Caroline and Grinnell. It has 300 first-come spaces, is open 24 hours except during evacuations, and takes cars, vans and passenger trucks only (height limit 7 ft 2 in, length limit 20 ft). As of the city page checked on 28 Nov 2026, published rates were $6.00 an hour plus tax and $48.00 a day plus tax (special-event rates may apply) — check official site before you rely on those figures.

Payment at the garage includes free rides on Key West city bus routes for everyone in the vehicle (not the Lower Keys Shuttle). Park once, walk Old Town, and use the bus. Overnight parking is allowed for passenger vehicles.
