Evidence checklist

  • Carrier written confirmation of the delay or cancellation and its cause.
  • Original and new itineraries; boarding passes / eTickets for both travellers.
  • Receipts: meals, hotel, transport, replacement tickets, essentials during baggage delay.
  • Baggage: Property Irregularity Report at the airport before leaving the baggage hall; bag tags.
  • Medical: reports, invoices, prescriptions.
  • Police report for theft or loss (Smartraveller: always get one).
  • Record of carrier compensation, vouchers or refunds received (insurers usually deduct these).
