Ticket conditions (generic, as printed)

  • Valid only on the train, date and accommodation type booked; non-transferable.
  • Proper identification required for all passengers (Amtrak ID).
  • Arrive 30 minutes before departure, or 60 minutes if you need ticketing or baggage assistance (At the Station).
  • Checked baggage check-in normally closes 45 minutes before departure at stations with checked baggage.
  • Carry-on: 1 personal item under 14 × 11 × 7 in and 25 lb, plus 2 bags under 28 × 22 × 14 in and 50 lb each, per passenger (Amtrak baggage).
  • If the train is cancelled you can modify the reservation in the Amtrak app or on Amtrak.com.
  • Use headphones for audio/video on board.

To-do for Person A and Person W

Plan B — delayed, cancelled or missed

1. Train 40 delayed

  • First: check the Amtrak app / Amtrak.com status and station announcements. If departure is delayed 1+ hour you may use the service-disruption rebook-or-refund options (Amtrak T&C).
  • Arrival is the next afternoon and the next booked leg in these PDFs is 25 Jan from New York, so a delay mainly affects your own Washington plans. Tell the Washington hotel if arrival slips past check-in.

2. Train 40 cancelled

  • First: ask Amtrak (app, station agent or 1-800-USA-RAIL) to rebook — Amtrak says it will make every reasonable effort to rebook on the next available service and may source alternate transport in significant disruptions (Amtrak commitments). Or take the refund.
  • Next same train: the Floridian runs daily (route page); the timetable shows train 40 leaving Tampa at 4:49 PM (timetable) — next day’s train 40, subject to space.
  • Silver Meteor: does not call at Tampa; it runs from Orlando/Kissimmee to Washington and New York. Amtrak’s Silver Meteor timetable lists a Thruway bus via Tampa to Orlando (timetable) — times and space: check official site.
  • Fly: Tampa (TPA) to Washington Reagan (DCA), Dulles (IAD) or Baltimore (BWI) — schedules and fares: check official site (airline sites).
  • Drive / rental car: one-way rental Tampa → Washington; one-way fees and drive time: check official site.

3. Missed departure

  • If you miss train 40 for your own reasons, the no-show rule forfeits the fare (Amtrak). If you know in advance, cancel before departure (30% forfeited on this fare type) rather than no-show.
  • Buy a new ticket for the next Floridian, or use the flight / drive options above.

Travel insurance / claims checklist

  • Tell the insurer’s 24-hour assistance line as soon as a delay, cancellation or missed connection changes plans (number kept offline — see the contact checklist).
  • Ask the carrier for a written delay or cancellation confirmation (reason, scheduled vs actual times).
  • Keep boarding passes / eTickets, the new itinerary, and every receipt (meals, hotel, transport, replacement tickets).
  • Note whether the cause was within the carrier’s control and whether you accepted rebooking, a voucher or a refund.
  • Log the claim on the insurance tracker (status, deadline, evidence).
  • Insurers often exclude delays caused by the transport provider and start delay cover only after a set number of hours — check your PDS (Smartraveller / CHOICE).

Official Amtrak policies (as published)

  • Fare conditions printed on the ticket: “VLD DATE TRAIN TICKETED; NO SHOW: FORFEIT VALUE”, “COACH: NO CHANGES, 30% CANCEL FEE”, “ADULT · FULL FARE · ID REQUIRED”. This matches what Amtrak’s refund policy calls a Value fare (no changes; 30% forfeited if cancelled before departure) — Amtrak refunds policy.
  • No-show: segments not cancelled before departure are forfeited (Amtrak).
  • Service disruption fare protection: when a ticketed departure is delayed 1+ hour, you may rebook once (within 7 days of the disruption, new travel within one month, same origin/destination, same reservation) or take a full refund of affected and remaining segments (Amtrak Terms and Conditions).
  • Cancellations by Amtrak / events beyond its control: refund to the original form of payment (Amtrak commitments).
  • Overnight disruption within Amtrak’s control: when available, Amtrak-contracted hotels or sleeper space; food and drinks when severely delayed or a guaranteed connection is missed (Amtrak commitments). Compensation, where offered, may be points, a transportation voucher and/or a refund of the affected segment — amounts are not published: check official site.
  • Where to act: Amtrak app or Amtrak.com, a station ticket agent, or 1-800-USA-RAIL (printed on the ticket) — see the contact checklist.
