San Francisco / Bay Area

4 days in SFO / Bay Area (13–17 Dec) · San Francisco visit plan

14.3°C (58°F) high / 8.3°C (47°F) low

Light-to-medium jacket, layers and a waterproof shell. Evenings are around 8°C. You will not need a heavy winter coat here, but keep one in the bag for Boise.

Snow is not expected.

December: 120.9 mm, 11.6 days with at least 0.25 mm. In this 5-day stay window, about 1.5 days with at least 1 mm.

In the 1991–2020 sample for these calendar days, one in ten nights was at or below 3.3°C (38°F), and the coldest night was 0.8°C (33°F). Average daylight: 9.6 hours.

Official source: NOAA 1991–2020 daily normals at San Francisco Downtown (Mission Dolores) (USW00023272), averaged over 13–17 Dec.

Boise

18 days in Boise (17 Dec–4 Jan) · Boise visit plan

3.1°C (38°F) high / −4.2°C (24°F) low

Full winter kit: insulated coat, warm layers, hat, gloves and waterproof winter boots. This is the longest stay, and cold snaps can drop well below −10°C. Pack this kit to reuse in Washington, New York and Chicago.

Snow is likely. Official normals: 14.2 cm (5.6 in) in December (5.5 days) and 13.5 cm (5.3 in) in January (5.2 days). In this stay window a measurable snow day occurred in 27 of 30 years in the 1991–2020 sample (about 5.3 snow days in the window).

December: 39.1 mm, 12.0 days with at least 0.25 mm · January: 35.8 mm, 11.1 days with at least 0.25 mm. In this 19-day stay window, about 6.1 days with at least 1 mm.

In the 1991–2020 sample for these calendar days, one in ten nights was at or below −12.6°C (9°F), and the coldest night was −21.1°C (−6°F). Average daylight: 9.0 hours.

Bangkok

Arrival after 31 Jan LAX departure (International Date Line); early February · Bangkok airport transit

33.7°C (93°F) high / 24.8°C (77°F) low

Hot and humid in the dry season (official February high 33.7°C, humidity around 71%). Light, breathable clothes, sun protection, and a layer for air-conditioned indoors. Expect a sharp change after the US winter. Haze is common (about 17 days in a typical February).

Dry season. Official February normal: 21.4 mm over 2.5 rain days. In the 1–7 Feb historical sample, about 0.6 days with at least 1 mm.

February rain: 21.4 mm over 2.5 days (a rain day is at least 0.1 mm). Mean humidity 71%. Sunshine 215.8 hours in the month. Record-ish February extremes in the same normals: 38.8°C (102°F) / 13.9°C (57°F). Haze days: 16.9.

Official source: TMD 1991–2020 February monthly normals, Bangkok Metropolis (TMD / WMO 48455). Open-Meteo 1–7 Feb window average in the same 30-year period: 31.5°C (89°F) / 22.4°C (72°F) (a little cooler than the full-month station normal).

Packing summary

Winter kit

  • Insulated winter coat, warm mid-layers, hat, gloves and a scarf — needed from Boise through Chicago (17 Dec–28 Jan).
  • Waterproof winter boots with grip, for snow and ice in Boise, Washington, New York and Chicago.
  • Plan for cold snaps, not just the average night: about one in ten Boise nights in this window were at or below −12.6°C (9°F), and the same figure is −14.8°C (5°F) in New York and −16.0°C (3°F) in Chicago.

Mild and warm stops

  • A medium, packable jacket for San Francisco, Las Vegas and Los Angeles, plus a waterproof shell for San Francisco’s wet season and an occasional January rain day in Los Angeles or Tampa.
  • Light clothes for Tampa (9–14 Jan) and Bangkok (early February).
  • Sun protection for Las Vegas, Los Angeles, Tampa and especially Bangkok (February high 33.7°C).
  • A light layer for air-conditioned indoor spaces in Bangkok.

How to pack the swings

  • The trip swings from sub-freezing Chicago to hot Bangkok in a few days. Pack the winter kit so it can be worn or packed away, rather than shipping it home mid-trip.
  • Tampa sits in the middle of the winter-city run, so keep a warm layer even there: nights average around 11°C and a rare cold front can go near freezing.
  • A live forecast is worth adding about 10–14 days before each leg, especially for snow in Boise, Washington, New York and Chicago.

When a live forecast can be added

Because the first legs are still more than two weeks away, a model forecast would not yet be reliable for packing. Around 10–14 days before San Francisco (and then before each later city) a live forecast can be added to this page, with a clear label that it is a forecast for the travel dates, not a climate normal.

Method

  • The stay dates are beyond a reliable forecast range, so this page uses climate normals — 30-year averages for the same calendar days — not a forecast for 2026–27. A given year can be warmer, colder, wetter or snowier.
  • For the eight US cities, the headline high and low are NOAA NCEI 1991–2020 daily normals, averaged over the exact stay dates, in degrees Celsius (Fahrenheit in brackets). Stations: San Francisco Downtown / Mission Dolores (USW00023272); Boise Air Terminal (USW00024131); Las Vegas Harry Reid International, formerly McCarran (USW00023169); Tampa International (USW00012842); Washington Reagan National (USW00013743); New York Central Park (USW00094728); Chicago Midway (USW00014819); Los Angeles Downtown USC (USW00093134).
  • Monthly rainfall, wet-day counts (days with at least 0.01 inch / 0.254 mm) and snowfall come from NOAA 1991–2020 monthly normals at the same stations. Chicago snowfall is from O'Hare (USW00094846), because Midway has no snow fields in this dataset; O'Hare’s late-January daily normals (−0.4°C / −7.8°C) are close to Midway’s (0.3°C / −7.3°C).
  • Window-specific wet-day counts (at least 1 mm), snow-day chance, the share of years with any snow, 10th-percentile and sample-minimum nights, and daylight hours are computed from the Open-Meteo Historical Weather API daily series, 1 January 1991 to 31 December 2020, at the city coordinates listed in the data file. That series is a historical reanalysis, not the official station normal, and runs a little cooler than NOAA at some cities (about 1–2°C, more on New York nights).
  • Bangkok headline figures are the official Thai Meteorological Department 1991–2020 February monthly normals for Bangkok Metropolis, station 48455 (13°43′N, 100°33′E), published in Climatological Data of Thailand for 30 Year Period (1991–2020), page 51: mean maximum 33.7°C, mean minimum 24.8°C, 21.4 mm of rain over 2.5 days (a rain day is 0.1 mm or more), mean humidity 71%, February extremes 38.8°C and 13.9°C. The Open-Meteo 1–7 February window average in the same 30 years is 31.5°C / 22.4°C — a little cooler, as expected for early February versus the whole month, and for a grid point versus the city station.
  • The temperature chart uses the official NOAA daily normal for each stay date at the US stations, and a flat official February monthly normal for Bangkok. 31 January is shown as a transit gap (LAX–BKK crosses the International Date Line; arrival is early February).
  • Current conditions were read from the Open-Meteo Forecast API on 27 Nov 2026, 8:14 PM AEDT (Melbourne time) and are labelled “today”, not as trip-date weather.
  • A live forecast can be added about 10–14 days before each leg, when model forecasts become useful, especially for snow in Boise, Washington, New York and Chicago.

Not collected

  • This is not a forecast for December 2026 or January 2027. Year-to-year swings, cold-air outbreaks, atmospheric rivers and snowstorms are not predicted here.
  • No hourly or packing-for-a-named-day forecast. A live forecast can be added about 10–14 days before each leg.
  • Florida was listed as “Florida” on the itinerary; Tampa is used because that city was confirmed. Other Florida cities would differ.
  • Bay Area microclimates (SF vs Oakland vs the Peninsula vs SFO airport) are not split out; the official figures are San Francisco Downtown. SFO airport is often a little cooler and foggier.
  • Train-car temperature on the NYC–Chicago trip was not collected; the Chicago figures are for outdoor conditions at Midway.
  • The exact Bangkok arrival clock time was not on the itinerary (LAX departs 8:00 AM on 31 Jan and the flight crosses the International Date Line), so Bangkok uses early-February / official February normals.
  • Air quality, UV index, wind-chill tables, and hotel or indoor temperatures were not collected. Bangkok’s official normals do include a February haze-day count (16.9 days), which is noted on the Bangkok card.
  • The Thai Meteorological Department website and climate API were not used for the figures: the public climate pages did not return usable data at the time of the pull (HTTP 500 / 403). The official 1991–2020 PDF hosted on a TMD subdomain was used instead. The TMD API catalogue lists an older 1981–2010 “ThailandClimateNormal” product, which was not used.
  • NOAA daily normals were requested in metric units. Monthly TAVG fields in the same feed arrived in Fahrenheit and were ignored; TMAX, TMIN, precipitation and snow were consistent with Celsius and millimetres and were the fields used.
