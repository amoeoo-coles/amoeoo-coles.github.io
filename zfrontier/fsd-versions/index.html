FSD versions

🛣️ FSD version tracking: US, UK, Australia

v2 · 26 Nov 2026, 8:45 AM AEDT

Tesla's FSD (Supervised) is driver assistance: the driver must stay attentive and remains responsible [1] [3]. The US has started a first-wave rollout of v15.0.1 to some HW4 Model Y cars; Australia is still on v14.3.12 for HW4 only, with no v15 date announced; and the UK has no approval for the supervised city system.

🇺🇸 United States

Available FSD (Supervised)

HW4 first wave: v15.0.1 on 2026.38.6.11 (first seen 21 Nov 2026; some Model Y cars)

HW4 wider fleet: v14.3.13 on 2026.38.5.13 (13 Nov) and 2026.44.1 (16 Nov)

HW3: v14.3 Lite (also on 2026.44.1)

[3] [5] [6] [7] [8] [4] as of 26 Nov 2026

🇦🇺 Australia

Available FSD (Supervised), HW4 only

HW4: v14.3.12 “Down Under” on 2026.38.5.5 (first seen 21 Oct 2026). No catch-up since 13 Nov

HW3: not available. No announced date for v15

[10] [11] [12] as of 24–26 Nov 2026

🇬🇧 United Kingdom

Not approved Supervised city system not approved in Great Britain

Paid package now Tesla Assisted Driving Basic (£99/month) with already-approved features only

[14] [15] [19] as of 23–26 Nov 2026

Tesla claim Tesla said on 21 Nov 2026 that “FSD v15 now starting to roll out” and that v15 improves the “critical intervention rate by 25x.” Elon Musk wrote on 20 Nov that “FSD V15 goes to wide release next month.” These are Tesla or Musk claims. Tesla has not published the method behind the 25× figure, and no independent verification was found. Musk gave no day and no country list. [3] [6] [12] [15]

Since 13 Nov 2026

What changed in the sources opened for this refresh, versus the v1 page (facts researched 13 Nov 2026):

  • United States. A first wave of FSD (Supervised) v15.0.1 on software 2026.38.6.11 began on 21 Nov 2026. Teslascope and FSD Update recorded the early installs they had seen on HW4 Model Y cars in the United States. Not a Tesla App tags the v15.0.1 notes for the new Model Y on HW4. FSD Update wrote that it had not seen a v15 or Lite build for HW3 cars. Teslascope, opened 26 Nov 2026, still listed v14.3.13 on 2026.38.5.13 (released 13 Nov) and on 2026.44.1 (released 16 Nov) as the wider active HW4 builds, with HW3 on v14.3 Lite. Tracker install shares differ and are not an official fleet figure. [3] [5] [6] [7] [8] [4]
  • Australia. No catch-up. Volts & Bots, updated 24 Nov 2026 at 6:14 am AEDT, still lists the latest verified ANZ FSD build as v14.3.12 on 2026.38.5.5, first seen on an Australian car at 1:20 pm AEDT on 21 Oct 2026. Tesla has not announced an Australia or New Zealand date for v15. EV Select (22 Nov) also found no public v15 build for Australia. Teslascope lists 2026.38.6.1 (released 29 Oct, still rolling in late November) as a minor-fixes update for HW3 cars in Australia and New Zealand, not a new FSD version. [10] [11] [12] [22]
  • United Kingdom. Still not approved. Arrival Notes (23 Nov) and Landing Soon (last checked 15 Nov) repeat that the supervised system is not approved in Great Britain, and that the VCA had undertaken no type-approval activity as of 9 Oct 2026 (as reported by EV Compared). No newer VCA statement was found in the sources opened on 26 Nov 2026. Arrival Notes cites a Department for Transport freedom of information reply that a new version of the UN driver-assistance rules (UN R171) is due to take effect on 27 December 2026. The Automated Vehicles Act 2024 is still described as fully in force in the second half of 2027. Neither is a UK FSD launch date. [14] [15] [19]
  • Europe (relevant). The latest named Tesla Assisted Driving build in approved countries remains 14.2.2.7 on 2026.39.100 (11 Nov), and Teslascope still listed that build as active in Europe when opened on 26 Nov. On 16 Nov the RDW approved a further TAD update for HW4 or higher: earlier attention warnings, a “full strike” if the driver is not paying attention during a manoeuvre, and a hands-on-wheel requirement above 130 km/h. The software build number for that update is not collected (not named by the RDW or by Electrek’s 18 Nov report). TeslAnt reported that 2026.44.1 reached many European cars that were not on the TAD build, and that none of the first 139 TeslaFi installs it counted had come from 2026.39.100. Arrival Notes, citing Highways News, says Italy stated on 20 Nov that it had recognised the Dutch approval. An EU-wide vote is still described as December 2026 at the earliest. Europe is not getting v15 in this US first wave; each European change needs approval first. [4] [18] [16] [17] [9] [15]

Not collected

  • The software build number of the TAD update the RDW approved on 16 Nov 2026.
  • A live price from Tesla’s own Australia website (A$149/month is from Volts & Bots, citing CarExpert; EV Select also could not check Tesla’s AU site).
  • A Vehicle Certification Agency statement later than 9 Oct 2026 — none was found in the sources opened on 26 Nov 2026.
  • Independent verification, or Tesla’s method, for the 25× “critical intervention rate” claim.
  • An official Tesla date for v15 in Australia, New Zealand, Europe or the UK, or for FSD (Supervised) Lite on HW3 cars in Australia and New Zealand.
  • An official US fleet-share figure for v15.0.1 (Teslascope and Not a Tesla App / TeslaFi counts differed when opened).
