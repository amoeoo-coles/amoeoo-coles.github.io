Official CES 2027 dates

CES 2027 takes place 6–9 January 2027 in Las Vegas, owned and produced by the Consumer Technology Association (CTA) (ces.tech; repeated on CTA press releases). Official CES 2027 Media Days are an exclusive media-only event on Monday 4 January and Tuesday 5 January, before the show opens (CTA, 17 Nov 2026). CTA says the show floor opens on 6 January at 10 AM PT. CES is described as celebrating 60 years of innovation in that Media Days release.

This hub also has a page for 3 January because a 3–6 January set was requested. Official sources retrieved on 21 Nov 2026 do not list 3 January as a Media Day or as a show-floor day. That is stated clearly on the 3 January page. Official show days 7–9 January are part of CES 2027 but are not given their own pages in this set.

Venues

  • Las Vegas Convention Center (LVCC) — North, South, Central and West Halls, plus outdoor spaces. Official 2027 hall themes are on the LVCC Campus page.
  • The Venetian Campus — smarter living, digital health, Eureka Park startups, and some keynotes (Venetian Campus).
  • Mandalay Bay — official home of Media Days events on 4–5 January (Tech Trends to Watch, Unveiled Las Vegas).
  • Fontainebleau Las Vegas — CES Foundry, 6–8 January, official hub for AI and quantum, “just steps from the Convention Center.”
  • ARIA — C Space activation hours are listed on the official transportation flyer; the WPP keynote is a C Space keynote at ARIA.
  • Sphere — venue named in CTA’s Lenovo keynote release for 6 January at 5 PM.

Registration

Registration is open (ces.tech). CES is a trade-only event for people 18 or older affiliated with the consumer technology industry. It is not open to the general public (Registration Information). Attendee, media and exhibitor personnel are separate registration types. CTA says Maritz is the only official registration vendor for CES 2027, and warns about registration scams.

Pass prices on the official registration page as of 21 Nov 2026: Standard Pass $149 through 30 Nov, then $349 from 1 Dec through 9 Jan; Premium Pass $1,450 through 30 Nov, then $1,750. Prices are in US dollars as shown and may change — check the official page when you book. Standard includes exhibit-floor access, keynotes, Great Minds sessions and select conference sessions. Cancellations received in writing by 9 Dec 2026 are refundable less a $50 processing fee; no refunds after that date, per the same page.

Main themes (official 2027 lists)

The official conference program says CES 2027 will highlight advancements in AI, enterprise innovation, health, energy future, gaming, manufacturing, robotics, retail, vehicle tech and advanced mobility, wearables and more. Paid tracks retrieved on 21 Nov 2026 include Robotics (full day, LVCC), Vehicle Tech and Advanced Mobility, Artificial Intelligence, XR and Spatial Computing, Quantum Means Business (full day, ARIA), Novaspace space technologies, and others. The program page does not assign those tracks to a calendar day.

Venue themes are summarised under Venues above. CTA has also announced a “largest mobility footprint ever” at CES 2027 (CTA mobility release, 2 Nov 2026).
