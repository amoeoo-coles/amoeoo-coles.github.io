Physical AI · 6 Jan

What official CES 2027 sources support for physical AI, embodied AI and related CES themes on 6 Jan 2027 (First official CES 2027 show day; show floor opens). Exhibitors and sessions are named only when a retrieved source names them. If a day-specific schedule was not published, this page says so.

What to look for

This is the first official show day. Hours: 10 AM–6 PM for the exhibit floor. Keynotes listed above on the date page also fall today.

CES Foundry, 6–8 January at Fontainebleau Las Vegas, is officially “the home for AI and quantum innovation at CES,” with hours 10 AM–6 PM on 6 January (CES Foundry). CTA’s Foundry materials describe AI “evolving from assistant to autonomous decision-maker” and mention robotics and mobility among the shift — that is a theme statement, not a 6 January session grid. The conference program also lists paid tracks in Artificial Intelligence (half day, LVCC) and Vehicle Tech and Advanced Mobility (full day, LVCC); the calendar day of each track is to be confirmed on the official schedule.

Stellantis is a sourced opening-day keynote (8:30 AM, Palazzo Ballroom) with booth 4518 in LVCC West Hall (CTA Stellantis release). The Waymo keynote was the previous evening, not today.

Where on the show floor (if sourced)

Official 2027 hall themes (LVCC Campus, Venetian Campus, CES Foundry): LVCC North (AI, energy, IoT, smart cities); Central (major brands, entertainment and smart home); West (mobility, including self-driving and, as CTA puts it, vehicles for agriculture, construction and space exploration); Fontainebleau CES Foundry (AI and quantum, 6–8 Jan); Venetian Campus (smarter living, digital health, Eureka Park). A booth number is given only when a named official or exhibitor source was retrieved.

Foundry hours today: 10 AM–6 PM at Fontainebleau, steps from LVCC West Hall. C Space at ARIA: 9 AM–6 PM activation hours on the transportation flyer (locations subject to change).

Sessions and announcements

Sourced today: show floor 10 AM–6 PM; Foundry 10 AM–6 PM; published keynotes at 8:30 AM, 11 AM, 2 PM and 5 PM (see the date page). Conference-track clock times and the exact day of the Robotics, Quantum, Novaspace and XR tracks were not on the conference-program page retrieved on 21 Nov 2026 — check the official CES Schedule.

Visit checklist

  • CES is trade-only and not open to the general public; carry a valid CES 2027 badge.
  • Show floor: 10 AM–6 PM today. Foundry (Fontainebleau): 10 AM–6 PM.
  • Recheck keynote rooms and any bag policy on ces.tech the same morning.
  • If you care about the Robotics, Quantum, Novaspace or XR tracks, confirm the calendar day on the official schedule — it was not listed on the program page retrieved on 21 Nov 2026. Official schedule pages retrieved on 22 Nov 2026 date some Robotics track sessions to 6 January (for example Humanoids Enter the Workforce, 2:00–2:40 PM, W219).
  • West Hall is the official mobility hall; Foundry is the official AI and quantum hub.
  • This site will not invent exhibitors or session times; confirm everything on ces.tech before you walk.
