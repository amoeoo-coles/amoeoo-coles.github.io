CES 2027 schedule — Las Vegas, 4–9 Jan 2027

Your schedule at CES 2027: show floor Wednesday 6 to Saturday 9 January, plus the pre-show day on Tuesday and your arrival night. Every day has an Option A (stay at the Las Vegas Convention Center) and an Option B (stay at the Venetian Expo). The two Next Wave of Physical AI tours are recommended for you; the tour dates and times are not published yet, so they say “times TBA”. Hours, keynotes, shuttles and badge rules are from official CES pages retrieved 28 Nov 2026. See also the CES 2027 hub and the Las Vegas trip page.

The venues

Each venue is a stop on the map above. Facts are from the official CES pages in the Sources.

Las Vegas Convention Center (LVCC)

  • Largest venue: North, South, Central and West Halls, plus Central Plaza and the Diamond Lot outdoors.
  • North Hall: enterprise innovation, Physical AI, robotics, automation, AI, energy, IoT and smart cities. Central Hall: big brands, entertainment and smart home. West Hall: mobility, from self-driving cars to farm, construction and space vehicles. South Hall: Design and Source.
  • Free LVCC Loop links West Hall with Central and South Halls. Monorail and Tech Express board near Central Plaza.

Official LVCC campus guide · OSM · Google

Venetian Expo

  • Level 1: Eureka Park, the startup hub (international pavilions and early-stage companies). Level 2: Digital Health, Wellness and Smart Living (wearables, food tech, age tech, beauty tech, smart home).
  • Also hosts some keynotes (Palazzo Ballroom at The Venetian) and the Startup Stage. Tech Express stops on Sands Ave in front of the Expo.

Official Venetian campus guide · OSM · Google

Fontainebleau Las Vegas — CES Foundry

  • CES Foundry, the AI and quantum home at CES, runs 6–8 Jan (10 AM–6 PM Wed; 9 AM–6 PM Thu and Fri; closed Sat). Official page: “just steps” from the LVCC; the West Hall shuttle stop also serves it.

Official Foundry page · OSM · Google

ARIA — C Space

  • C Space (advertising, entertainment and content): 9 AM–6 PM Tue–Fri (closed Sat). WPP keynote Wed 6 Jan, 11 AM, Mariposa Ballroom at ARIA.
  • Reached by the C Space Shuttle (Route 7) from the LVCC and Venetian Expo, Wed–Fri.

Official C Space page · OSM · Google

Sphere — Lenovo Tech World keynote

  • Lenovo Tech World @ CES, Wed 6 Jan, 5 PM PT. Attendance is limited to credentialed CES attendees; CTA says ticketing and logistics details are still to be announced.

CTA Lenovo release · OSM · Google

Mandalay Bay — Media Days (media only)

  • Media Days are 4–5 Jan: Tech Trends to Watch (4 Jan, 3 PM) and CES Unveiled Las Vegas (4 Jan, 4–7 PM). Open exclusively to credentialed media, content creators and analysts. Not part of an attendee plan.

CTA Media Days release · OSM · Google

Harry Reid International Airport (LAS)

Day-by-day plan with Option A and Option B

Times are Pacific. “TBA” means CES has not published a date or start time for that item. Where a session has an official date and room, it is listed with a link to its page. Official session lists are first-come, first-served.

Mon 4 Jan · Arrival night

Your flight from Boise lands at 9:15 PM Pacific. The show floor is closed. Media Day 1 at Mandalay Bay is credentialed media only, so skip it unless you hold a Media badge.

Option A

Pick up your badge at the airport tonight

Badge pickup is open in baggage claim 7 AM–midnight Mon–Wed

  • Terminal 1, Terminal 3 (United) and Terminal 3 International all have a desk; use the one for your airline's terminal (not known yet).
  • Bring government photo ID and your registration QR code (wallet pass or confirmation email). You can only collect your own badge.
  • Then hotel and rest: Tuesday is a light day.

Check: Landing at 9:15 PM leaves under three hours before midnight; desk queue lengths are not published.

Badge pickup locations and hours · OSM · Google

Option B

Straight to the hotel; badge on Tuesday

Lower pressure after a late flight

  • Collect your badge Tue 5 Jan from 7:30 AM at the LVCC (Central Hall) or from 8 AM at The Venetian Theater Entrance or ARIA.
  • You do not need the badge for Tuesday’s city option; you need it for the Insider Briefing and the keynote.

Check: The airport desks close at 7 PM on Thu and are closed Fri and Sat; venue desks cover the rest.

Badge pickup locations and hours

Tue 5 Jan · Pre-show day: floor closed

Exhibits are closed. Official hours: conference programming 10 AM–5 PM, C Space 9 AM–6 PM (conference programming only). Media Day 2 is media only. One evening keynote: Waymo, 6:30 PM, Palazzo Ballroom at The Venetian.

Option A

LVCC and the Insider Briefing

Official: CES Insider Briefing: Decoding the Show Floor, Tue 5 Jan, 2:30 PM, LVCC, $99

  • Seated panel on the key trends and exhibitors to watch: no walking. Moderated by Lori Schwartz with Brian Comiskey and Chris Dethloff.
  • Morning: badge pickup at the LVCC if you have not collected it, then pick your Wednesday tours and sessions.
  • 6:30 PM: Waymo co-CEO Tekedra Mawakana keynote at The Venetian (Palazzo Ballroom). Take a rideshare or the monorail then walk; Tech Express runs 8 AM–6 PM on Tuesday.
  • Hub context: see the CES 2027 hub for the media-day and keynote summary.

Check: The Insider Briefing is a separate paid ticket ($99) listed on its own page; check how it is added to your registration.

Option A

LVCC until close, then the airport

Rolling bags are not allowed in CES venues

  • Check out in the morning and leave bags with the hotel, or use CES bag check: outside LVCC Central and West Halls.
  • Spend the day on whatever you flagged in North, Central and West Halls; floors close at 4 PM.
  • Evening: rideshare from the LVCC (pickup outside South Hall or in the Diamond Lot by West Hall) back for your bags, then to the airport.
  • CES’s $10 airport shuttle runs Saturday noon–5 PM only, too early for a 10:55 PM flight.

Check: Leave extra time for the Saturday airport run.

Registration, badge pickup and bag policy

You need an approved badge: CES is trade-only

CES is for people 18 or older affiliated with the consumer technology industry; it is not open to the public. Registration needs proof of industry affiliation and employment (see the official page). Standard Pass: $149 through 30 Nov, then $349 (1 Dec–9 Jan); Premium $1,450 through 30 Nov, then $1,750. Standard includes the exhibit floor, keynotes, Great Minds and select conference sessions. Cancellation in writing by 9 Dec 2026 is refundable less $50; no refunds after. Maritz is the only official registration vendor: beware scams. Official registration information · Register.

Badge pickup

  • Badges are never mailed. Pick up your own badge before entering any show venue; bring government photo ID and your registration QR code. No group pickup and no pickup for someone else.
  • One badge per person; no substitutions for credentialed attendees.

Bags and security

  • Two small bags per person, each smaller than 12 × 17 × 6 inches. Clear bags (mesh, plastic, vinyl) speed entry.
  • Not accepted: rolling bags of any size (luggage, carry-ons), laptop and computer bags, and rolling luggage carts.
  • All bags are searched at every venue entrance; expect visible law enforcement and K9 units. Use the pedestrian walkways.
  • Bag check: outside LVCC Central and West Halls; Venetian Expo Level 1 lobby near Hall G; Venetian Ballroom. CES encourages leaving bags at your hotel.

Other practical tips

  • Use the official CES 2027 app for maps with blue-dot positioning and the security contacts.
  • Weather: a medium jacket is enough (usual highs near 14 °C, nights near 4 °C); see the Las Vegas trip page weather section.
  • Conference sessions are seated first-come, first-served; paid tracks show “Purchase Track”.
  • Distances are big: the LVCC campus plus the Venetian is a lot of walking. Wear comfortable shoes and plan around the shuttles above.
