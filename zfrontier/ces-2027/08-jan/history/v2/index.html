CES 2027 · 8 January · Foundry last day

v1 · 22 Nov 2026, 7:56 AM AEDT

Friday, 8 January 2027 is the third official show day of CES 2027. Official exhibit hours are 9 AM–6 PM. It is also the last published day of CES Foundry at Fontainebleau (9 AM–6 PM). The official show continues on Saturday 9 January, when exhibit hours are 9 AM–4 PM and Foundry and C Space are listed as closed. The official Keynotes schedule retrieved on 22 Nov 2026 lists no keynote on 8 January.

Show day · Foundry last day Third official CES 2027 show day, and the last published day of CES Foundry. Official exhibit hours are 9 AM–6 PM (CES 2027 transportation flyer, as of 29 Sep 2026; also CES Dates and Hours). This is not the last day of the show: Saturday 9 January remains an official exhibit day (9 AM–4 PM), with Foundry and C Space listed as closed.

Hours and other venues (as published)

  • CES exhibit hours, 8 Jan: 9 AM–6 PM.
  • CES Foundry at Fontainebleau: 9 AM–6 PM on 8 Jan, the last of the published 6–8 January Foundry days (CES Foundry; hours also on the transportation flyer).
  • C Space at ARIA activation hours, 8 Jan: 9 AM–6 PM on the transportation flyer (locations subject to change).
  • Saturday 9 January (no page in this set): exhibit hours 9 AM–4 PM; Foundry and C Space closed on the Dates and Hours page.

Published keynotes on 8 January

The official CES 2027 Keynotes schedule retrieved on 22 Nov 2026 lists no keynote on Friday 8 January. The page says “stay tuned for more announcements.” Any later keynote added for this day is not collected here until it appears on that official page.

Official track notes for this day

CTA’s 2 Nov 2026 mobility release lists the Resilient Communities track on Friday 8 January (CTA mobility release). Individual session titles in that track were not collected unless they also appear on an official CES Schedule page cited below.
