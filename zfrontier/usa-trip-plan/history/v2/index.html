USA Trip Plan

v2 · 26 Nov 2026, 8:36 PM AEDT

This hidden page maps the trip legs that were provided, in the numbering given. Leg 1 was not provided and is shown as not collected. Legs 2 and 3 are ticketed flights; ticket details were not collected. Leg 4 is a flight to Tampa International Airport. Legs 5 and 6 are drives. Leg 7 is a train from New York to Los Angeles via Chicago; the routing is assumed as Amtrak’s Lake Shore Limited (NYP–CHI) then the Southwest Chief (CHI–Los Angeles Union Station). Dates and durations for the trip are not collected.

The Los Angeles train marker is Los Angeles Union Station. Amtrak’s station code for that station is LAX, which is not the same place as Los Angeles International Airport (IATA code LAX).

Map

Numbered markers follow the collected stops in itinerary order, starting at SFO (the origin of leg 2). Flights are dashed great-circle arcs. Drives follow a computed road route from the OSRM public demo router, fetched once while building the page and embedded here. Trains follow OpenStreetMap relation geometry. All route lines are static JSON in this page; only map tiles, comments and the visitor badge are requested in the browser.
